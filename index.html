How to play

In VR

  • Push floors, walls and logs with your hands to move. Fling your arms to jump.
  • Swing your hand fast into an ape to slap it. Faster swings hit harder.
  • Enough slaps make an ape dizzy and floppy. Hold grip near an ape to pick it up, then swing and let go to throw.
  • Stab an ape while it's dizzy and it's wrecked: out for the round, and its body stays on the map. Grab a body with grip and swing it at people, or throw it.
  • Smash crates that drop in to find tools: mallet, frying pan, plunger, rubber chicken, knife, boxing-glove launcher and banana peels. Hold grip to hold a tool, swing to hit, let go to throw. Trigger fires the glove launcher.
  • Your fingers follow the controller: the trigger curls your pointing finger, grip curls the rest (and grabs), and your thumb drops when it rests on the buttons.
  • Grabbed? Keep slapping the ape holding you until it lets go. Dizzy? Shake your arms hard to snap out of it.
  • Hold grip on a ledge or beam to hang on. Right stick turns.
  • Fall into the ravine and you're out until the next round. Watch out for the swinging log.

On a computer

  • WASD to move, Space to hop, mouse to look, click to slap, hold right-click to grab and release to throw.
