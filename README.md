# WRECKED

A VR ape brawler prototype for Meta Quest (WebXR). Move by pushing the world with your arms, slap other apes until they're dizzy, then grab them and throw them off the Ravine Sawmill. Last ape standing wins.

**Play:** https://oliverbell369.github.io/ape-rumble/ — open it in the Meta Quest Browser and press **Enter VR**. It also runs in a desktop browser (WASD, mouse, click to slap, hold right-click to grab).

Grabbed? Keep slapping the ape holding you until it lets go (3 slaps, or 2 hard ones). Dizzy? Shake your arms hard (or mash keys on a computer) to snap out of it.

**Online:** every game is online. Leave the room code empty for Quick Play, or type the same code as your friends to play together (up to 6 players; bots fill empty seats). Multiplayer runs on Photon Realtime; the room's first player hosts the bots and the round clock.

**The Yard:** everyone arrives in the Yard, a lobby on the canyon rim. Mess around, punch the punching bag, check yourself in the mirror, slap in a room code on the keypad, then stand on the START pad (or slap the red button) to begin a best-of-3 match. After the match everyone returns to the Yard.

Hands have three moving fingers: the trigger curls your pointing finger, grip curls the rest (and grabs), and your thumb drops when it rests on the buttons. Other players see your fingers move.

**Crates and tools:** crates drop in during fights; slap one open for a mallet (squashes apes flat), frying pan, plunger (sticks and drags), rubber chicken, knife (stab them and they bleed), boxing-glove launcher (trigger) or banana peel. Hold grip to hold a tool, swing to hit, let go to throw. There is a tool rack in the Yard to try them, and a Blood effects switch on the menu.

Prototype 0.7.
